# NxtWave AI Workshop — 7-Day Growth Plan (500 Registrations)

**Campaign Goal:** 500 final-year engineering student registrations in 7 days  
**Budget:** ₹2,000 | **Campaign Window:** 7 Days | **Asset:** "Campus Squad" Registration Engine  

---

## 1. Target Audience & Core Value Proposition
- **Target Student:** Final-year (and pre-final year) engineering students (CSE/IT/AIML/ECE/EEE) across Tier-2 and Tier-3 Indian colleges.
- **The Core Anxiety:** Placement season is imminent, technical interview bars have risen due to AI, and their resume currently only has generic boilerplate projects (e.g. basic e-commerce or library management).
- **The Hook:** **"Build Your First AI Project in 60 Minutes. Free. Live. Beginner-friendly."**
- **Conversion Triggers:**
  1. *Zero barrier to entry:* 100% free, zero installation (cloud-based).
  2. *Resume credibility:* Ready-to-paste GitHub repo + verifiable digital certificate.
  3. *Social proof / FOMO:* College leaderboard showing where their campus ranks.

---

## 2. The 3 Prioritised Channels (Target: 500 Registrations)

| Channel | Projected Regs | Conversion Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| **Channel 1: College WhatsApp Network (CRs & Club Leads)** | **300 (60%)** | Partner with Class Representatives (CRs) and coding club leads across 30 Tier-2/3 campuses. Provide ready-to-forward WhatsApp broadcast cards with unique trackable UTM links (`/?utm_source=whatsapp&campus=srm-ap`). One forward in an active class group of 70 students yields 10–15 registrations. |
| **Channel 2: College Club Outreach & Placement Cells** | **100 (20%)** | Direct outreach to AI/ML clubs, GDSC leads, and department placement coordinators with an official "60-Min AI Upskilling" endorsement kit and invitation message. |
| **Channel 3: The Campus Squad Viral Referral Engine** | **100 (20%)** | Every registrant automatically gets a unique referral code and 1-tap WhatsApp share link. Tiered incentives: 3 referrals unlock the **AI Project Starter Kit**, 10 referrals enter the **₹1,000 Voucher Prize Pool**. Live college leaderboard fuels inter-college rivalry. |

### What We Are Deliberately NOT Doing (And Why):
- **NO broad-scale paid ads:** ₹2,000 split across Meta/Google ads yields high CAC (>₹50/lead) with poor qualification and low show-up rates.
- **NO cold email scraping:** Leads to spam flag, low open rates (<12%) among Indian students who predominantly live on WhatsApp and Instagram.
- **NO complex prerequisites:** No local Python or CUDA installations required; eliminating drop-offs before the workshop begins.

## 5. Funnel Math & Conversion Benchmark

- **Link Impressions Required:** ~5,000 views across WhatsApp forwards, LinkedIn posts, and club emails.
- **Landing Page Visitors (Unique):** 2,500 (50% CTR from messages).
- **Form Completion Rate:** **20%** (2,500 visitors × 20% = **500 Registrations**). Minimal-friction form (name, email, WhatsApp, college autocomplete).
- **Show-Up Rate Mitigation (Industry benchmark is 30–40%):**
  - **T-24 Hours:** WhatsApp reminder with workshop calendar invite (.ics).
  - **T-1 Hour:** Direct WhatsApp & SMS ping: *"Your cloud workspace is spinning up — join here"*.
  - **Incentive:** Certificate only awarded to live participants who submit the verification token.

## 6. Contingency Plan: What If We Are Behind at Day 3?
If cumulative registrations are under 150 by Day 3 evening:
1. **Activate "Inter-College Pride Challenge":** Announce that the college with the highest registrations gets a sponsored offline AI Hackathon or exclusive kit for their coding club.
2. **Lower Referral Threshold:** Instantly reward anyone who brings 2 friends with the "AI Resume Cheat Sheet & Prompt Bible".
3. **Double-Down on Class Reps:** Offer a ₹100 direct mobile recharge voucher to CRs whose section achieves >25 registrations.
