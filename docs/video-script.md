# 3-Minute Video Script — NxtWave Growth Intern Challenge

**Presenter:** Growth Strategist & Full-Stack Engineer  
**Tone:** Confident, structured, first-person, metrics-driven  
**Total Duration:** 3:00 (approx. 400 words)  

---

### [0:00 – 0:30] Problem & Student Psychology
*"Hi, I’m Siddarth. When final-year engineering students look at their resumes today, there’s one glaring anxiety: technical placements are getting harder, mass recruiters have raised the bar, and almost every student’s resume still has the same generic library management or e-commerce project.*

*They want to build with AI, but they’re stuck in tutorial hell — overwhelmed by complex math or heavy installations. Our mission was to solve this: get 500 final-year students registered in 7 days on a strict ₹2,000 budget for a free 60-minute masterclass: 'Build Your First AI Project in 60 Minutes'."*

---

### [0:30 – 1:30] The 7-Day Growth Plan
*"To hit 500 registrations without spending money on expensive paid ads, we prioritized 3 high-leverage organic channels:*

*First, our primary channel driving 300 registrations: College Class Representatives and coding club leads across 30 campuses. We built pre-crafted, 1-tap WhatsApp broadcast cards with unique trackable UTM links.*

*Second, campus coding and placement cells, contributing 100 registrations through our ready-to-endorse upskilling kit.*

*And third, our secret weapon: 'Campus Squad' — our viral referral engine, contributing the remaining 100 registrations. Out of our ₹2,000 budget, we put ₹1,000 directly into a top-referrer prize pool, ₹500 into micro-targeted copy tests on Instagram, and ₹500 into domain and infrastructure buffers.*

*Funnel math is clear: 5,000 impressions → 2,500 visitors → at a 20% conversion rate on our streamlined form = 500 confirmed registrations."*

---

### [1:30 – 2:30] Product Demo: The "Campus Squad" Engine
*(Screen shares the live platform)*

*"Here’s the live asset we built. Notice the mobile-first, high-contrast dark aesthetic with zero heavy 3D assets to guarantee sub-second mobile load times.*

*Right on the hero, students see live social proof: 487 seats filled, an urgency countdown, and an inline fast-registration card. We seeded an autocomplete dropdown of over 100 top Indian engineering colleges — from IITs and NITs to SRM and VIT — reducing friction to under 30 seconds.*

*Down here, we showcase the 3 tangible projects: an AI Campus Assistant with RAG, an AI Resume Scorer, and a Multimodal Vision app, complete with ready-to-paste resume bullet points.*

*The moment a student registers, they unlock their personal Campus Squad referral link. With 1-tap WhatsApp sharing, they can invite classmates to unlock an AI Starter Kit at 3 referrals or enter the prize pool at 10 referrals, tracked on a live college leaderboard."*

---

### [2:30 – 3:00] Learnings & Next Steps
*"What did we deliberately avoid? We rejected heavy 3D animations that kill mobile performance, and we rejected email OTP gates that create drop-off. By focusing on minimal friction and peer distribution, we built an engine that scales organically.*

*If I had 24 more hours, I'd plug in automated WhatsApp webhooks to boost show-up rates from 40% to 75%.*

*The platform is live, connected to a cloud database, and demo-ready. Thank you!"*
